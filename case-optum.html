Optum · Campaign · with Bolster

Have the foresight to eliminate hindsight

A B2B campaign for healthcare's least glamorous, most consequential software — built on the gap between fixing a problem and never having it.

Hero image TK — campaign key visual
01 — The situation

Optum Claims Manager sits inside health systems doing work nobody outside billing thinks about — catching errors in claims before they go out the door. The category sells itself on accuracy rates and integration specs, to an audience that has heard every accuracy rate before.

The buyer is a revenue cycle executive who has been burned. That's the only thing you can count on about them.

02 — What I noticed

Nobody buys claims software to fix claims. They buy it to stop being surprised.

Which moved the whole argument off features and onto a feeling — the specific dread of finding out about a problem too late to do anything about it. Once you name that, the product stops being software and starts being the absence of a bad afternoon.

03 — What we made

The line — Have the foresight to eliminate hindsight — and the campaign built around it. Foresight and hindsight are the two states the buyer lives between, so the headline does the positioning work before anyone reads a word of body copy.

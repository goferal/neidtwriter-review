Work, sorted by problem.

Not by client, and not by deliverable. What matters is what kind of problem it was — because that's what tells you whether I'm any use to yours.

The three kinds
01 — Foundations

Brand platforms, positioning, naming, architecture, messaging systems. The thing everything else gets built on top of.

02 — Campaigns

Go-to-market, integrated, advertising. Taking a platform all the way out to where actual people meet it.

03 — Content & Film

Long-form, editorial, scripted video. Usually where the volume lives, and where a platform proves it can hold up.

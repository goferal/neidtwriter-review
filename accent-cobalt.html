01 — What I do

I build brand foundations — positioning, architecture, platforms, the sentence a company has to be able to repeat — and then prove them in language precise enough to survive contact with a 60,000-product manufacturer.

Independent since 2013, working directly with brands and alongside agencies like BBDO, Wolff Olins and Publicis. Creative director and brand strategist, with a writer's ear for the difference between true and almost true.

Best with what I'd call craft clients — any size, any industry, as long as they're genuinely invested in the thing they make.

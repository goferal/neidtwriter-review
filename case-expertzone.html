Microsoft · Foundations

ExpertZone

A brand and training platform for the people who actually sell Microsoft — and who almost nobody at Microsoft was writing for.

Hero image TK
01 — The situation

Microsoft spends enormously on marketing to consumers and to enterprise IT. In between sits a third audience it doesn't employ and can't directly manage: the retail floor staff at Best Buy and every other store, who answer the actual question customers ask — which one should I get?

Training content for that audience is usually written like a compliance module. It gets clicked through, not read.

02 — What I noticed

The people selling your product to customers were the only audience nobody had written for.

Not written at — written for. A retail associate isn't a channel to be pushed through; they're a person on their feet for eight hours who wants to sound like they know what they're talking about. Make them feel expert and they sell for you. Make them feel trained and they don't.

03 — What we made

Brand and voice for the ExpertZone platform, plus the content system that let it keep producing — the same pattern as 3M, at a different scale: build the language, then build the thing that lets other people use the language without you.

01 — Situation

Growth by acquisition left LiftOne with a unified name and a fractured identity. They needed to rally an expanded internal team and define exactly what the company stood for.

02 — Connection

Nobody falls in love with a forklift. What the audience cares about is what happens when one stops moving in the middle of a shift.

03 — What we made

A cinematic brand anthem that skips the corporate specs to focus on human commitment. "We Promise" grounds LiftOne's identity in the quiet grit and daily reality of the people operating the equipment. And the simple, durable promise of the team backing them up.
